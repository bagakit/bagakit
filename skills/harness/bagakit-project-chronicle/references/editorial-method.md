## Pass 1: Session Cards

Read each included session on its own terms before comparing it with later
sessions. Capture:

- original intent and constraint
- actions actually taken
- observed outcome
- turning points
- belief corrections
- quality, friction, and cost leverage
- counterevidence or unresolved ambiguity
- bounded locators for every important claim

Avoid hindsight language in this pass. A session does not “foreshadow” a later
invention until lineage synthesis establishes that connection.

## Pass 2: Lineage

Build epochs from changed capability baselines, not date buckets.

A generation boundary is justified when at least one inheritance changes:

- what the Agent can reliably do
- what the project treats as source of truth
- which failure is now caught earlier
- what humans must explain or approve
- how much time, context, or tool work a repeated task costs
- which uncertainty becomes explicit instead of hidden

Use this causal chain:

```text
belief -> pressure -> intervention -> observed evidence -> revised principle -> ratchet
```

Where causality is uncertain, record a sequence or correlation instead of a
cause. Preserve regressions and abandoned branches; a generation can inherit a
warning as well as a victory.

Each epoch should answer:

1. What was the prior operating world?
2. Which assumption or bottleneck defined its limit?
3. Which sessions exposed the limit?
4. What changed in behavior, tools, contracts, or evidence?
5. What became possible or cheaper afterward?
6. What debt survived into the next generation?

## Pass 3: Dramatic Casting

Cast sessions by the function they played in the lineage. A session may carry
more than one role, and several sessions may share one role.

Useful archetypes:

- Pathfinder
  - exposed a route or possibility before it was reliable
- Smith
  - turned insight into a reusable tool, contract, or workflow
- Sentinel
  - revealed a hidden failure, contradiction, or unsafe assumption
- Cartographer
  - made state, evidence, ownership, or boundaries legible
- Steward
  - reduced maintenance cost and protected long-term coherence
- Envoy
  - translated one system's evidence into another system's contract
- Challenger
  - overturned an attractive but wrong prior belief
- Heir
  - inherited earlier work and proved transfer in a new setting

Invent project-specific epithets when they are more evocative, but always
record:

- operational function
- member session ids
- why the casting fits
- supporting evidence refs

Do not assign motives, emotions, heroism, blame, or consensus that the sources
do not establish. Roles describe contribution to the story, not human worth.

## Pass 4: Harness Evolution

Extract only lessons that may change future choices. Classify entries as:

- `success-principle`
  - what worked and the mechanism that made it work
- `corrected-belief`
  - an initially plausible belief revised by execution evidence
- `quality-ratchet`
  - a check, oracle, review, or artifact that prevents silent regression
- `friction-lever`
  - a change that reduces coordination, ambiguity, or repeated explanation
- `cost-lever`
  - a change that lowers time, context, tool, or maintenance cost
- `unresolved-tension`
  - an important conflict not yet ready for generalization

For each entry preserve:

- `what`
- `why`
- `intended_generalization`
- `failure_boundary`
- `behavior_examples`
- `transfer_checks`
- `evidence_refs`
- `counterevidence_refs`
- epistemic status and confidence

Reject slogans. “Validation improves quality” is not useful until the record
names which failure becomes observable, through what proof surface, and where
the approach does not transfer.

## Pass 5: Epic Editing

Write the chronicle after the structured lineage and ledger exist.

Use epic devices that preserve truth:

- recurring symbols for persistent constraints
- role epithets linked to the cast ledger
- reversals grounded in corrected beliefs
- inheritance between epochs
- debts, fractures, and unfinished quests for open problems
- concise scene-setting drawn from observable project conditions

Avoid devices that blur truth:

- invented dialogue or inner thought
- compressed composite events presented as one session
- stronger causal language than the evidence supports
- victories measured only by agent confidence
- villains created from failed attempts or people
- a clean upward arc that hides regressions and contradictions

Use citations or compact evidence notes without turning the story into a lab
report. The appendix ledger carries the full analytical burden.

## Review

Ask seven questions:

1. Coverage honesty: Does the census bound every completeness claim?
2. Evidence fidelity: Can each material claim resolve to a card and locator?
3. Contradiction handling: Are incompatible observations visible and disposed?
4. Epic without fabrication: Can every flourish be separated from factual
   content without changing the claim?
5. Generational delta: Does each epoch change an inherited capability baseline?
6. Harness value: Could a future Agent act differently from the ledger?
7. Privacy and retention: Does the run avoid raw or unauthorized content?

Mark the review accepted only when every gate passes. A beautiful chronicle
with weak evidence remains a draft; a perfect ledger with unreadable prose
remains an unfinished chronicle.
