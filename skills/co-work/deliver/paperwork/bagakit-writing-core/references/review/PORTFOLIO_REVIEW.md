# Portfolio Review

Use this review only when two or more related articles will be drafted or
released as a batch. It is an advisory about editorial structure, not a rule
that every article must look different.

## Before drafting

For each article, record:

- `artifact`: the article being reviewed
- `unique_promise`: the one judgment this article must deliver
- `spine`: the sequence of paragraph or section functions that carries that
  judgment
- `shared_constraints`: source, audience, format, or procedure constraints that
  may legitimately repeat

Describe the spine by function, such as `failure -> mechanism -> repair`, not
by a list of literal headings. This makes template reuse visible without
mistaking shared vocabulary for shared structure.

## Review

1. Compare the functional spines, not just headings or word choice.
2. Separate repeated domain logic from repeated editorial scaffolding. A shared
   procedure order or evidence boundary may be necessary; a shared sequence of
   “main judgment -> topics -> exclusions -> comparison -> takeaway” across
   unrelated subjects is a review signal.
3. Check whether each article has its own opening move, evidence order, turning
   point, and ending. If not, change the spine before polishing sentences.
4. Keep the finding advisory. Record the reason for any shared structure and
   the smallest variation that restores each article's promise.

## Review output

Record in the Core review packet:

- each article's `unique_promise` and `spine`
- the shared structure observed
- whether the repetition is justified by constraints
- the per-article differentiator to preserve
- disposition: `keep`, `vary`, or `rebuild_spine`

Do not use this review for unrelated drafts, API/reference formats, or
procedure/runbook documents whose order is part of the contract. Do not turn
structural variety into a new template requirement.
