# Pilot Calibration

This pilot maps sanitized serious-moment outcomes to structured Project
Chronicle guards. It does not use phrase overlap, a model judge, or a single
literary-quality score.

Calibration rules:

- pass only when the expected evidence is observable in the route or artifacts
- fail a polished story that weakens census, source, contradiction, or
  promotion boundaries
- treat `should_not` cases as negative controls
- allow different prose, epoch counts, and role names when the guarded behavior
  is preserved
- do not reward more ceremony, more chapters, or more dramatic language by
  itself
- require human calibration before a future subjective or model grader becomes
  trusted optimization evidence

Current pilot status:

- all cases are synthetic or sanitized and contain no raw transcript
- baseline and holdout splits both contain positive and negative cases
- every row declares success evidence, a transfer limit, and structured guard
  ids
- three trials with a minimum pass rate of `0.67` are requested for future
  live-agent runs
- no eval score is release-blocking or promotion authority
